About the author

Hi, I’m Mark Hedquist

I’ve worked in the corporate world for three decades. I now serve project & program managers, organizational PMOs and executives to build cultures rooted in trust, empathy and service. I founded On Becoming a Servant Leader to share the tools, skills, mindset and behaviors that helped me and others to lead with greater purpose and impact.

Whether you are new to your project or program role or a seasoned project leader, my goal is to support you to develop your servant leadership and emotional intelligence skills to enable you to gain executive presence and help you achieve a fulfilling career at work and at home.
